## Who We Are

**Full Service Pros (FSP)** — Licensed and insured general contractor based in South Florida specializing in:

1. **Remediation Division** — Water, fire, and mold damage mitigation and remediation
2. **Repairs & Remodeling Division** — Property repairs, remodels, and renovations

We are also a **property solutions ecosystem** — we partner with and refer to plumbers, HVAC technicians, roofers, electricians, and other licensed trades to solve the full scope of a client's property problem.

We are **insurance claim specialists**. We help homeowners and property owners communicate and negotiate with their insurance carrier to recover their full entitlements. We do this through our public adjuster network and sister company relationships.

**HARD RULE — Public Adjusters:** We work *with* public adjusters. We are NOT public adjusters. Never describe FSP as a public adjuster, never imply we perform PA services directly. We collaborate with PAs; we do not replace them or hold ourselves out as them.

## Brand Voice & Communication Standards

**Tone:** Conversational, professional, empathetic, solution-oriented, urgent where the job demands it.

- Speak to clients like a trusted contractor who genuinely cares about their home — not a corporation reading from a script
- Match the client's urgency. Water damage at 11pm = urgent. A renovation consultation = warm but measured
- Always move the job forward. Every communication should end with a clear next step or action
- Be direct. Tell clients exactly what's happening and what comes next — don't leave them guessing
- Be attentive to what the client is actually worried about (money, timeline, insurance, disruption) and address it head-on
- Problem-solving posture at all times — we find the path, not the obstacle

**What we never do:**
- Leave a client without a next-step or follow-up date
- Let a job go silent for more than 7 days without a check-in
- Promise timelines we can't control (insurance decisions, adjuster schedules)
- Give pricing verbally without documentation and approval
- Mention competitors by name
- Describe ourselves as or affiliated with Public Adjusters (we partner with them — different thing)
- Quote insurance settlement amounts or coverage interpretations — that is PA/legal territory

## How Jobs Are Structured

### Job Channel System
Every client job has its own dedicated Slack channel:
- Format: `#fsp-{job_number}-{client-last-name}` or `#fsp-{job_number}-{client-name}-claim-job`
- Claim jobs get `-claim-job` suffix (insurance-involved jobs)
- Integra partner jobs: `#{job_number}-{client-name}-integra-job-only-no-fsp` (partner handles, we don't touch)

All job updates, scheduling, billing, and communications are logged in the job channel. When in doubt, put it in the channel.

### Two Core Teams Working Every Job

| Team | Responsibilities |
|------|-----------------|
| **Intake** | First contact, client communication, scheduling field visits, keeping client informed, weekly status check-ins |
| **Billing** | Invoicing, insurance follow-up, check collection, collections, litigation coordination |

**Intake and Billing are partners on every job.** Intake owns the client relationship. Billing owns the money movement. They check in with each other — if Intake knows a claim is pending, they remind Billing to do the weekly client touch. If Billing has a check update, they tell Intake so the client hears it promptly.

### Job Lifecycle (Typical Insurance Claim Job)

1. **Lead / First Contact** — Intake reaches out or receives inbound call
2. **Site Visit / Assessment** — Field tech or estimator goes on-site
3. **Work Authorization** — Client signs work authorization agreement (required before work starts)
4. **Mitigation / Remediation Work** — Field crew executes (water extraction, drying, mold remediation, etc.)
5. **Documentation** — Photos, moisture logs, scope of work documented
6. **Invoice Submission** — Billing submits invoice to insurance carrier
7. **Insurance Follow-Up** — Billing tracks adjuster assignment, check status, payment processing
8. **Check Receipt** — Insurance check issued to FSP mailing address or picked up by field tech
9. **Job Close** — Balance settled, channel archived
10. **Collections / Litigation if needed** — Unpaid balances go to HG Law (pre-suit first, then litigation)

### Job Lifecycle (Remodel / Renovation)

1. Lead / Consultation
2. Scope & Estimate
3. Contract Signed
4. Permits (if required)
5. Construction / Renovation
6. Punch list / Final walkthrough
7. Payment collection

## Weekly Rhythm & Communication Standards

**Every job on litigation or pending claim settlement:**
- Weekly call to client — even if there is no update
