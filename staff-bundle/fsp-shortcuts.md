# FSP Agent Shortcuts
# Quick-access Claude agent shortcuts for FSP staff
# Installed to: ~/.claude/fsp-shortcuts.md
# Use in any Claude session by typing the shortcut phrase

---

## Universal Shortcuts (All Staff)

| Say this | What Claude does |
|----------|-----------------|
| `fsp job update [channel/client name]` | Drafts a professional status update for the job channel |
| `fsp client call [situation]` | Scripts a client phone call or voicemail for the given situation |
| `fsp weekly check-in [client name]` | Drafts the standard weekly litigation/pending claim check-in message |
| `fsp email [purpose] to [client/party]` | Drafts a professional email following FSP brand voice |
| `fsp review request` | Drafts a natural, non-pushy Google/BBB review request to send to client |
| `fsp summary [paste channel messages]` | Summarizes a job channel into status + open items + who owns what |

## Hard Rules Claude Always Follows for FSP

These are non-negotiable regardless of who's asking:

1. **Never describe FSP as a Public Adjuster** — we partner with PAs, we are not PAs
2. **Never quote coverage amounts or predict insurance outcomes** — factual documentation only
3. **Never promise timelines on insurance decisions** — carriers set their own timelines
4. **Never waive or adjust invoice amounts** — escalate to billing management
5. **Flag sensitive scenarios** with [SENSITIVITY CHECK] rather than deciding alone
6. **Always include a next step** in any client-facing draft
7. **Never give legal advice** — route to HG Law / Cohen Legal
8. **Always CC Jordan or Mark** on any communication involving litigation, pre-suit, or settlement decisions
